About Me

Building practical cybersecurity skills through experience, projects, and continuous learning.

I’m Alexander Cruz, a cybersecurity student focused on penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, security research, and technical problem-solving.

My approach to cybersecurity is simple: learn it by doing it.

I’m currently studying Cybersecurity at Fisher College in Boston, where I’ve been building a strong foundation across networking, secure software development, operating systems, information security, databases, and programming.

Outside the classroom, I focus heavily on hands-on security work. I spend time working through penetration testing labs, vulnerability assessments, TryHackMe training, security research, and independent technical projects.

What interests me most is understanding how systems can fail, how those weaknesses can be validated safely, and how the results can be translated into clear recommendations that actually help an organization improve.
