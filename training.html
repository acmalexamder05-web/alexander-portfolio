My Learning Process

Training is only the first step.

I try to convert guided learning into independent technical ability by documenting what I learn and then applying it in separate assessments.

01

Learn

Understand the concept, tool, or vulnerability through structured training.

02

Practice

Use the technique inside authorized lab environments and understand what the output means.

03

Document

Record commands, findings, lessons learned, risks, and remediation guidance.

04

Apply Independently

Use the skill later without relying on walkthroughs or predefined answers.
